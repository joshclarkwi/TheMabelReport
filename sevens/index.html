      The Rules

      1. Two to eight players. The deck is dealt out as evenly as it goes, so with an awkward number some hands carry one extra card. The deal rotates, so that falls on somebody new each time.
      2. Whoever holds the seven of spades opens with it.
      3. Thereafter you may play a seven of any suit not yet begun, or a card that extends a suit already on the table by one rank, up or down.
      4. Each suit fans out from its seven — down toward the ace on one side, up toward the king on the other.
      5. You must play if you can. Passing is not a choice; when you have no legal card the table passes you automatically and play carries on round.
      6. First to shed every card wins the deal.

      The whole art of it is which end you feed. Advance a suit you can follow yourself and you keep your options; advance one you have nothing left in and you have simply helped everybody else.

      Computer players are marked ◇. The host may seat up to seven of them, and may start the game whenever they please without waiting for a full table. Should a player's telephone go quiet mid-game, the house plays their hand until they return.
