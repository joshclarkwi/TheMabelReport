Appendix

The Rules

  1. The deck is split evenly — twenty-six cards apiece.
  2. Whoever holds the seven of diamonds opens with it.
  3. Thereafter you may play a seven of any suit not yet begun, or a card that extends a suit already on the table by one rank, up or down.
  4. Each suit fans out from its seven — down toward the ace on one side, up toward the king on the other.
  5. You must play if you can. Passing is not a choice; when you have no legal card the table passes you automatically and your opponent goes again.
  6. First to shed every card wins. The cards left in the other hand are the margin.

The whole art of it is which end you feed. Sit on a seven and you lock a suit — but you lock your own cards behind it too.
